## Core teaching (from the AMN Zones Guide)

1. **Always trade with the trend.** Downtrend → only supply zones; uptrend →
   only demand zones. Counter-trend setups drastically lower edge.
2. **Break of Structure (BOS) zones.** Identify the last move (up-to-down or
   down-to-up) that caused a clear BOS. These mark where institutions
   participated → high-probability zones.
3. **Liquidity & the First Tap.** Before price hits a zone, look for a
   liquidity sweep. The first tap should react quickly, leaving most of the
   zone unmitigated for a stronger next entry. This confirms intent and
   preserves edge.
